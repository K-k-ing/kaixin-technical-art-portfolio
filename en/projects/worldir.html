01 / Why WORLDIR is needed

A plausible image
is not enough to keep production moving.

An early Astra scene captured the relationships between a corridor, windows, and equipment, but the foreground equipment's volume and occlusion diverged from the reference. In production, that can obstruct circulation or make an asset unsuitable for reuse—even when it looks convincing.

Reconstruction introduces another problem: missing floor sections and deformed surfaces. Copying the source geometry copies its errors; filling gaps by appearance can turn unknowns into supposed observations. Uncertain depth, scale, cameras, and occluded geometry need explicit records and decisions.

WORLDIR's approach is to separate proposals from accepted scene state: record evidence and candidates, check structural and provenance constraints, then execute approved decisions. It is a scene-organization and compilation prototype, not a general-purpose exact reconstruction system.

Understanding / Reconstruction tools

Provide spatial and semantic candidates

Tools such as Astra and Marble provide layouts, relationships, reconstructed representations, and cues; they may also output structured data. Their reliability needs to be assessed against each source.

Asset generation tools

Provide candidate geometry and appearance

Tripo / Hyper3D and WorldSculpt provide object candidates. A complete-looking asset still needs orientation, scale, and scene-fit checks.

WORLDIR

Organize decisions and execute them

Retain sources and reasons, isolate unaccepted candidates, check their support, and compile approved scene decisions into an execution plan.

02 / Input—decision—output

Establish what has support.
Then decide what belongs in the scene.

This is a responsibility chain, not a demonstration that every stage is connected in one click. Early experiments used real references, reconstructions, and generated assets; v2 currently verifies interfaces and compilation rules with fixed synthetic inputs.

Models propose. Scene Graph records accepted decisions. Compiler executes.
v2's fixed inputs are the Scene Graph revision, Asset Manifest, Compile Policy, and Compiler Version. Determinism means consistent Build Plans for identical inputs in a pinned environment—not deterministic generative models or cross-platform byte identity.

What can a user work with?

Structured decisions, provenance chains, compilation diagnostics, and execution plans. The bounded scenes demonstrated so far can be inspected, edited, and compared in Blender; v2 save-and-reopen checks read actual scene state. Free edits require renewed review rather than reuse of an old validation result. The complete real-asset-to-Unreal delivery chain is still in development.

04 / Three layers, three meanings

More complete does not mean more accurate.

Source

A model's spatial representation

Gaussian, PLY, and proxy representations can themselves contain gaps or deformation. They are evidence to assess, not measured real-world ground truth.

Reconstruction

A source-supported result under current rules

Retain what has sufficient source support, and preserve unknowns. Observed means supported within the source reconstruction, not verified by real-world measurement.

Production

Explicitly approved design completion

Add traceable production choices to the reconstruction. It may become more complete, but that does not increase the geometric authority of design inference.

Confidence describes the strength of support; authority defines what a source is allowed to decide. A high score cannot replace source permissions or an acceptance record.

05 / Second case · Early asset-fitting experiment

Reconstruction is not production geometry.

If a generated asset looks suitable, can it be placed correctly back into the scene? The chair case explores candidate fitting and human acceptance; the floor case verifies decisions and instancing. They are not a completed automatic chain.

The blurred regions in these views are a retained Gaussian-scene reference layer, used to help assess object positions, scales, and spatial relationships—not the final generated geometry. As individual 3D assets are progressively generated, validated, and placed, this reference layer can be hidden at any time with a single toggle, leaving a structured, editable 3D scene.

Return to the scene—not just the isolated asset.

After refitting, 90.7% of held-out surface samples fell within 0.05 source units, and position, orientation, and uniform-scale checks passed. I manually reviewed the candidate, judged it acceptable, and approved in-place replacement.

The log also records a manual adjustment to the acceptance threshold and remaining local backrest and view-ray depth discrepancies. The earlier unapproved diagnostic and subsequent scene review remain visible here, separating the reported fit metric from case-specific manual acceptance.

Secondary experiment / WorldSculpt: diagnosing fragmented output and restoring continuous surfaces

The early GLB loaded correctly but consisted of many disconnected fragments. I traced the failure to a checkpoint key mismatch and applied runtime key remapping with strict loading guards; the external reconstruction model is WorldSculpt. The repair produced more continuous surfaces, with thin sheets, cracks, and residual debris still present.

WorldSculpt is an archived experimental branch, outside the default pipeline. Dense reconstruction can provide geometric evidence, but compute cost, thin structures, topology, UVs, and production materials still limit direct use as real-time assets.

06 / My implementation, evidence, and current limits

From experimental observations
to reviewable implementation.

Early cases explain why this system is needed; the v2 rebuild uses clearer data contracts to verify execution. Their tests, datasets, and capabilities are recorded separately.

My specific contributions

  • Designed the separation of scene evidence, candidate proposals, and accepted decisions; implemented versioned records, field-level adoption, and provenance / asset checks.
  • Wrote Python compilation and validation prototypes that convert fixed scene state into a Build Plan, handling units, coordinates, pivots, hierarchy, and stable IDs.
  • Diagnosed missing reconstruction and candidate fitting in early experiments; established prototype / pattern / Station Graph rules and explicit design-completion policies.
  • Implemented Blender consumption, saving, fresh-process reopening, and independent readback checks, while retaining failures, environment blockers, and unrun items.

External models and tools: Astra supports understanding and candidate construction; Marble provides source reconstruction; Tripo / Hyper3D provide generated assets; WorldSculpt supplies the experimental reconstruction model. Blender and Unreal are downstream tools. My work is the workflow, data contracts, diagnosis, rules, integration, and validation.

Experimental

Real spatial evidence and asset fitting

Early floor decisions / instancing and chair fitting have their own evidence; additional camera registration remains ambiguous. Current artifacts do not establish a connected v2 workflow with real providers, real assets, and the older corridor.

Recorded source-space results and consistency with source geometry do not replace independent measurement, generalization evaluation, or exact collision checks.

Planned / Pending verification

Complete production delivery chain

Actual v2 Unreal execution, general asset resolution, real provider integration, automatic UV / PBR processing, and end-to-end delivery acceptance remain pending. Current Core checks structural / provenance constraints; it does not perform general hard-constraint solving.

Blender linked-library fixtures, forced termination during build / save, and OS-level access tracing remain NOT_RUN. Local test passes do not establish production readiness or completed generalization.

Historical spatial queries / Gaussian and pixel-to-XYZ evidence

500k Gaussian splats describes the scale of that early dataset. Of 20 query points, 17 received support and three returned unsupported; this is not scene accuracy.

Distances use source units. Occlusion, camera uncertainty, depth ambiguity, and missing rear geometry still require treatment.

WORLDIR × MATE / Integration direction

Connect scene decisions to asset-surface iteration.

WORLDIR organizes what a scene needs and why; MATE supports asset review, material binding, and editable surface iteration. They are currently independent prototypes. Automatic requirement handoff and scene feedback remain an integration direction.
