About me

I'm Kian (Kaixin) Kang, an undergraduate in Public Art at Sichuan Fine Arts Institute, focused on AI-assisted asset production and technical art tools. I enjoy turning specific production needs into tools artists can use, inspect, and refine.

Since 2023, I've used Stable Diffusion for novel illustrations, character design, and commissioned illustration, building experience in prompt design and style control. That work now extends into 3D: from AI concepts and Tripo modeling to Blender cleanup and validation in UE5 scenes.

In August 2026, I joined NetEase Games' ArtSpark team as a Technical Art / AI Pipeline intern. I contribute to a multimodal AI workflow canvas, ComfyUI image-to-image workflows, and stylized track and asset integration. I also develop Unreal Editor Python orbit-capture tools for 3DGS workflow validation. In my personal projects—MATE, Track Assembly, and Face Value—I explore material control, procedural assembly, and geometry budget planning.
